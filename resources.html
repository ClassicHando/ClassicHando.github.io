Resources

During my time as a counselor, I educated people of all age groups and walks of life from teen to elderly and it felt like nobody really had education in this area (I know I didn't), yet we expect people to navigate the world of personal finance. My goal is to consolidate the best FREE resources and education I can into one place and hopefully have a tool that will help with that.

If you have resources you would recommend, please contact me! Details are on the About page.
